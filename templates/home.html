About Smart Campus ERP

Smart Campus ERP System is a centralized web-based platform designed to automate and simplify the management of academic and administrative processes in colleges and educational institutions.

The system provides separate portals for students, teachers, and administrators where they can manage attendance, assignments, study materials, tests, results, and class schedules efficiently. The goal of this system is to reduce manual work, improve communication, and provide a smart digital environment for campus management.

Features Overview

📚 Student Portal

Students can access study materials, view assignments, check test results, see their timetable, and track their academic progress in one place.

👨‍🏫 Teacher Portal

Teachers can mark attendance, upload study materials, create tests, manage assignments, and publish results for their students.

👨‍💼 Admin Dashboard

Administrators can manage students, teachers, classes, subjects, and view analytics and reports for the entire campus system.

Core System Modules

📊 Attendance Management

Teachers can mark daily attendance and students can track their attendance records easily.

📝 Assignment Management

Teachers upload assignments and students can view or submit their work through the system.

📚 Study Materials

Teachers can upload lecture notes, PDFs, and learning resources for students anytime.

📈 Results & Performance

Students can view their test results and performance reports in a simple dashboard.
